# OneWishes — The Chronicle Archive Design System (Stitch Vintage)

## Brand Philosophy & Aesthetic Movement
**Tactile Skeuomorphism & Curatorial Nostalgia**
OneWishes is an eternal archival monument designed as an authentic late-19th/20th century archive. It rejects flat, sterile modern web abstractions in favor of tangible physical artifacts: aged tea-stained parchment, molten carmine beeswax seals, weathered brass brackets, typewriter strike-on letters, and authentic postal cancellation marks.

## The Four Sacred Historical Epochs
1. **1890s Victorian Wax-Sealed Epistle**: Deckled vellum paper, iron gall cursive ink, cracked molten carmine wax seals (`#7D1D23`).
2. **1930s Western Union Telegram**: Manila ticker paper (`#F0E2C8`), monospaced capitalized strikes, punched edge ribbon holes, `STOP` dividers.
3. **1950s Par Avion Transatlantic Airmail**: Iconic diagonal red & blue chevron piping (`#A62626` & `#1D3557`), airmail stamps, 3D flippable postcards.
4. **1970s Instant Polaroid Film**: Classic thick white-bordered photo cards with chemical developing emulsion and handwritten ballpoint captions.

## Typographic Hierarchy
- **Narrative & Classical Manuscript Headings**: `EB Garamond` (weights 400, 500, italic cuts for emotional resonance)
- **Mechanical Machine Strikes & Postal Metadata**: `Courier Prime` (fixed-width typewriter monospace)
- **Modern Supporting Labels**: `Inter` / system sans-serif

## Component Specifications
1. **Navbar**: Floating mahogany bureau capsule with rotating brass 35mm reel emblem, typewriter links, and audio synthesizer toggle.
2. **Favicon & Logo**: Intricate vintage brass infinity signet crest impressed into carmine sealing wax.
3. **Loading Screen**: Authentic darkroom developing bath with ticking analog clock and wax seal impression.
4. **Keepsake Cards**: Double-bezel construction with physical brass corner brackets (`vintage-brass-bracket-*`) and postal cancellation postmarks.
5. **Interactive Rituals**:
   - Seal fracture sound & parchment unfold on letter click.
   - 750Hz Morse code audio ticker on telegraph keys.
   - 180° 3D card flip on airmail postcards.
   - Chemical emulsion develop fade on instant photos.
